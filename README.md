The **McDonald's Sales Analysis** project is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze restaurant sales performance, profitability, product category distribution, and customer demographics.

The project uses sales data from multiple McDonald's restaurant locations to identify business trends and generate meaningful insights through Excel Pivot Tables, KPI analysis, and visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales across different states.
- Identify high-performing restaurant locations.
- Analyze month-wise sales trends.
- Understand product category performance.
- Analyze customer distribution by gender.
- Evaluate quantity sold across different product categories.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** 3,552,023
- **Total Profit:** 1,369,249
- **Average Sales:** 177.60

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. State-wise Sales Analysis
- Compare total sales across different states.
- Identify top-performing states.
- Analyze geographical sales distribution.

### 2. Month-wise Sales Analysis
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Compare sales performance across different months.

### 3. State-wise Profit Analysis
- Compare operating profit across states.
- Identify high-profit and low-profit regions.

### 4. Category and Gender-wise Analysis
- Beverage
- Burger
- Dessert
- Fries
- Sides
- Customer order distribution by Female, Male, and Other categories.

### 5. Category-wise Quantity Analysis
- Analyze total quantity sold by product category.
- Compare category-wise product demand.
- Identify popular product categories.
